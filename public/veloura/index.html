Our Story

A Legacy of Flavor, Crafted with Passion

Since 2010, Veloura has stood as an ode to the art of fine dining — a place where every plate tells a story and every evening becomes a cherished memory. Founded by executive chef Adrien Laurent, our kitchen honors seasonal produce sourced from partner farms within 100 miles of our door.

Ours is a philosophy of restraint and generosity: fewer ingredients, treated with reverence, elevated with technique honed over decades in Paris, Kyoto, and New York.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before your visit — from dress code to dietary requests.

Do I need to book in advance?

Yes, reservations are highly recommended. We open bookings up to 60 days ahead.

Is there a dress code?

Smart elegant. Jackets are recommended for gentlemen; sneakers and athletic wear are not permitted.

Do you accommodate dietary restrictions?

Absolutely. Please note allergies during booking — our chefs craft custom vegan, gluten-free and kosher menus.

Do you offer private dining?

Yes. Our two private suites host 8–20 guests with dedicated servers and custom menus.

Is parking available?

Complimentary valet parking is provided every evening from 5:30 PM.

Are children welcome?

Guests over the age of 8 are welcome in the main dining room; families are ideal for our Sunday service.

What forms of payment do you accept?

All major credit cards, contactless payment, and digital wallets. Cash is accepted for gratuities.

Do you have a cancellation policy?

Cancellations require 24 hours' notice. Chef's Table reservations require 72 hours.

Can I purchase a gift card?

Yes — physical and digital gift cards are available in any denomination from $50.

Do you host weddings and events?

We host intimate weddings, launch dinners and corporate events. Please contact our events team.
